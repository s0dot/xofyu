# Project ALGEBRAIC — *Algebraic!*

An **Adventure Time–themed battlegrounds fighting game for Roblox**, in the spirit of *Jujutsu Shenanigans*:
- you roll ("make Wishes") for **Fighting Styles** inspired by show characters, with seven rarities from Common to a secret Cosmic tier
- you brawl in hand-painted Land of Ooo arenas that transform every season

## Status

**v1.0, first complete draft (October 2026), for greenlight review.**

Research covers the battlegrounds genre, Roblox's 2026 platform and policy changes, *Adventure Time* lore and art direction, and Roblox engineering practice. It is summarized in §2, with sources in Appendix E.

> ⚠️ *Adventure Time* is owned by Cartoon Network / Warner Bros. Discovery. This document recommends a license-ready, IP-separable build and **no public launch of a monetized unlicensed version**. See §5.
